# A109 · Production Readiness Review

| Level | Topic | Estimated time | Read first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Expert | Capstones | 1–2 days | [Module 33 · Production Readiness](../02-modules/33-production-readiness.md) |

## Scenario

An app team says their service is ready for production. Review it.

## Your tasks

1. Take any sample microservice
2. Assess it against the production readiness checklist in Module 33
3. Fix every gap you can
4. Write a readiness report with risks you accept

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every checklist item marked pass, fixed or accepted risk
- [ ] Fixes merged
- [ ] Report readable by a non-Kubernetes manager

<details>
<summary>Hints (try without them first)</summary>

- Be specific: link to the YAML line for each item

</details>

## Stretch goal

Turn the checklist into automated policy checks.

## Submit

Push your manifests, scripts and a short `NOTES.md` (what you did, what broke, what you learned) to your own repository. Share it on LinkedIn and tag **DevOps Shack** if you'd like feedback.
